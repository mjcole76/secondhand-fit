## How the selected concept aligns [AI Analysis]
- **Technological Implementation: Strong** — Engineering a readiness pipeline backwards from the Apparel VTO endpoint's documented input constraints, calibrated by running degraded inputs through the real API, is exactly the kind of non-trivial, evidence-backed integration the criterion describes.
- **Design: Strong** — Seller capture with specific retake instructions, a published readiness grade, and a buyer render carrying that grade as a trust badge form one coherent two-sided product.
- **Potential Impact: Strong** — Resale listings genuinely break the assumptions of catalogue-grade try-on tooling, and the pre-flight grade addresses that specific failure for a clearly named audience on both sides.
- **Quality of the Idea: Strong** — Making the pre-flight photo-readiness grade the headline instead of the render is a non-obvious inversion, and it targets a segment the analyzed sample assumes away.
- **Stage One viability screen (pass/fail): Strong** — Apparel VTO is the core engine of an eCommerce workflow, placing it directly on the eCommerce VTO track.
