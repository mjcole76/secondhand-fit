# Contest Contract — YouCam API Skin AI & eCommerce VTO Hackathon

## Contest Overview
We’re unlocking our most advanced, market-tested AI and AR infrastructure just for you. It's time to build the real-world, scalable products, including cutting-edge Agentic AI workflows.
- Sponsor: Perfect Corp
- Prize pool: $6,000

## Eligibility
- [Mandatory] Entrants must be individuals at least the age of majority where they reside as of the time of entry, teams of such individuals, or organizations legally organized/incorporated at the time of entry.
- [Mandatory] Participants must be above the legal age of majority in their country of residence.
- [Potential Risk] Residents of, or organizations domiciled in, listed excluded countries/territories may not participate or receive a prize (including Afghanistan, Antarctica, Belarus, Brazil, China, Crimea, Cuba, Djibouti, Donetsk, Iran, Iraq, Italy, North Korea, Luhansk, Quebec, Russia, Somalia, Sudan, Ukraine, Venezuela, Vietnam, Western Sahara, and any other comprehensively OFAC-sanctioned country).
- [Potential Risk] Employees, agents and representatives of Promotion Entities (Sponsor/Administrator), judges, companies employing a judge, their immediate family/household members, and affiliates are not eligible.
- [Potential Risk] Any individual or organization whose participation would create a real or apparent conflict of interest, in the Sponsor's sole discretion, is not eligible.
- [Mandatory] A team or organization must appoint and authorize one individual (the Representative), who must meet the eligibility requirements, to enter the Submission on their behalf.
- [Optional] An Eligible Individual may join more than one Team or Organization and may also enter individually.

## Deadline
2026-11-02 06:00 UTC — as written: "Submission Period: Sep 29, 2026(12:00 pm Eastern Time) – Nov 2, 2026(11:45 am Eastern Time) (“Submission Period”)."

## Required Technologies
- Devpost — Registration and submission platform for the hackathon.
- Perfect Corp. YouCam API — At least one YouCam API must be integrated into a working web or mobile application; judging references the Skin, Beauty, Fashion, Jewelry & Watch, Hair & Beard categories.
- YouCam API account (yce.perfectcorp.com/ai-api) — Entrants must create a free YouCam API account and redeem the code for 1,000 free API units.
- YouTube (or Vimeo; Youku per contest page) — Demo video must be publicly hosted; YouTube highly preferred.
- Public or shared code repository (e.g., Git hosting) — A repository URL with all source code, assets and instructions must be provided; specific host is not named.

## Mandatory Features or Integrations
- Entrants must create a free YouCam API account at https://yce.perfectcorp.com/ai-api (separate link for the India region).
- The Project must be a working application that integrates at least one Perfect Corp. YouCam API and demonstrates clear consumer or eCommerce value.
- The solution must be a functional web or mobile prototype integrating at least one YouCam API; judging references APIs from the Skin, Beauty, Fashion, Jewelry & Watch, Hair & Beard categories.
- The Project must install and run consistently on its intended platform and function as depicted in the video and/or text description.
- Projects must be newly created, or if pre-existing, significantly updated after the start of the Submission Period; Entrants must explain how the Project was significantly updated during the Submission Period.
- If a Project integrates any third-party SDK, API and/or data, the Entrant must be authorized to use them under the tool's terms, conditions or licensing requirements.
- Use of the YouCam API is subject to YouCam's Terms of Service and Perfect Corp.'s Terms of Service.

## Judging Criteria
- Technological Implementation — How thoroughly and skillfully does the project integrate at least one Perfect Corp. YouCam API from the Skin, Beauty, Fashion, Jewelry & Watch, Hair & Beard category? Does the project demonstrate clear consumer or eCommerce value? Does the code reflect genuine effort and a working, non-trivial implementation?
- Design — Does the project deliver a complete, coherent product experience — not just a technical proof of concept?
- Potential Impact — Does the project make a credible, specific case for solving a real problem for a real audience — and does the solution actually address that problem based on what's demonstrated?
- Quality of the Idea — Is this a creative, non-obvious use of at least one Perfect Corp. YouCam API from the Skin, Beauty, Fashion, Jewelry & Watch, Hair & Beard category — and does the team demonstrate genuine understanding of the problem space?
- Stage One viability screen (pass/fail) — Pass/fail gate determining whether the Project reasonably fits the theme and reasonably applies the required APIs/SDKs featured in the Hackathon.

## Submission Assets
- New build, or documented significant update made during the Submission Period [Mandatory]
- Code repository URL provided with full source, assets and setup instructions [Mandatory]
- Repository is public with relevant licensing, or private and shared with contact_event@PerfectCorp.com [Mandatory]
- Text description explaining features, functionality and consumer/eCommerce value [Mandatory]
- Screenshots of the project included [Mandatory]
- Demo video between 1 and 3 minutes showing the experience end-to-end [Mandatory]
- Video explains which YouCam API(s) are used [Mandatory]
- Video includes footage of the Project functioning on its target device [Mandatory]
- Video publicly hosted on YouTube (preferred) or Vimeo with link on the submission form [Mandatory]
- Video free of unlicensed third-party trademarks and copyrighted music/material [Mandatory]
- Working demo accessible free of charge to judges until the end of the Judging Period [Mandatory]
- All submission materials in English (or with English translation) [Mandatory]
- Submit all required fields on the 'Enter a Submission' page before Nov 2, 2026 11:45 am ET [Mandatory]
- Opt in to Special Awards at submission if eligible [Optional]

## Disqualification Risks
- Residents of, or organizations domiciled in, listed excluded countries/territories may not participate or receive a prize (including Afghanistan, Antarctica, Belarus, Brazil, China, Crimea, Cuba, Djibouti, Donetsk, Iran, Iraq, Italy, North Korea, Luhansk, Quebec, Russia, Somalia, Sudan, Ukraine, Venezuela, Vietnam, Western Sahara, and any other comprehensively OFAC-sanctioned country).
- Employees, agents and representatives of Promotion Entities (Sponsor/Administrator), judges, companies employing a judge, their immediate family/household members, and affiliates are not eligible.
- Any individual or organization whose participation would create a real or apparent conflict of interest, in the Sponsor's sole discretion, is not eligible.
- The demo video must not include third party trademarks, or copyrighted music or other material unless the Entrant has permission to use it.
- The Submission must be the Entrant's original work, solely owned by the Entrant, and must not violate the intellectual property or other rights (copyright, trademark, patent, contract, privacy) of any other person or entity.
- Submitted content must not contain viruses, Trojan horses, worms, spyware or other disabling or malicious code.
- A Project must not have been developed with, or derived from a project developed with, financial or preferential support from the Sponsor or Administrator (e.g., funding, investment, contract work, or a commercial license) prior to the end of the Submission Period; such projects may be disqualified.
- Sponsor/Administrator may disqualify any entrant found tampering with the entry process or acting in violation of the Official Rules, inappropriately, unsportsmanlike, or in violation of law.
